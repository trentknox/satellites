AI Agent Decision Logs: Record Evidence Without Storing Chain of Thought

Published 2026-10-10 05:50 UTC · Source: DEV Community

AI agents that record decision evidence without storing chain-of-thought solve a real privacy problem. The approach: log what the agent decided and why, not the raw reasoning trace. This keeps you compliant while still debugging agent behavior.

Connect Your Business APIs Once, Share Them with Every Agent Client via MCP

Published 2026-10-10 04:31 UTC · Source: DEV Community

Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets you connect business APIs once and share them across every agent client — Claude, Cursor, Copilot, and custom agents. Instead of writing a new integration per client, you write one MCP server. This is the fastest way to make your tools agent-ready.

Your Agent Remembers the Stack, Not You

Published 2026-10-10 04:06 UTC · Source: DEV Community

AI coding agents should remember your technology stack so you don't have to repeat it every session. The fix: a persistent stack profile that the agent loads at startup. This eliminates the "we use Postgres 16, Tailwind, and Vite" preamble from every conversation.

StoreBench: A Live-Commerce Environment for Evaluating and Training Autonomous Operator Agents

Published 2026-10-10 04:00 UTC · Source: arXiv cs.AI

StoreBench provides a live e-commerce environment for testing autonomous agents that operate real stores. Agents must handle inventory, pricing, and customer interactions. The benchmark measures success rate, revenue impact, and recovery from errors.

Verification and Self-Improvement in Agentic AI: Foundations and Limits

Published 2026-10-10 04:00 UTC · Source: arXiv cs.AI

Verification in agentic AI systems: how do you know an agent did the right thing? This paper covers the foundations of agent verification, self-improvement loops, and the fundamental limits of each approach. Key insight: verification must be external to the agent's own reasoning.
